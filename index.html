About Galvanised Shamba Wire Products

Kenya's reliable partner for steel & construction supplies.

For over 15 years, Galvanised Shamba Wire Products has supplied premium fencing materials, galvanized wires, gabions, nails, and authentic water tanks to contractors, farmers, and households across Kenya.

We source directly from leading manufacturers, guaranteeing quality, competitive pricing, and fast nationwide delivery. From a single roll of barbed wire to a full construction site supply, we deliver with the same commitment to integrity.
